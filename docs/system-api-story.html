How Cool-Aid uses the System API

Cool-Aid tells a clinic which patients are at risk in a heat wave because of their medicines and their home. Every risk claim rests on published evidence. The System API is where we found that evidence, and how we checked it.

The data

Five kinds of data, joined into one evidence base

System is one of five sources. The others add place, regulation and patients. They connect on paper IDs, medicine group, ZIP code and date.

System Public Graph

  • Live API: 40.3M studies, 5.9M findings, 627k relationships
  • Harvard Dataverse v2.1 bulk release: 2.0M findings, 845k studies
  • PubMed for publication dates (1,207 papers)

Medical literature

  • PubMed + Europe PMC: 5,995 unique records, 1,965 chased citations
  • Screened to 333 included papers; 83 papers cited in the app

FDA & guidance

  • FDA drug labels: 5,938 sections audited, 299 heat sentences found
  • FDA adverse-event reports (21M) compared against control drugs
  • 13 heat-health guidance documents from CDC, NHS, Health Canada and others

Place & climate

  • National Weather Service HeatRisk; NOAA station history (183 Bronx heat waves)
  • Heat Vulnerability Index, Social Vulnerability Index, air-conditioning access
  • 1,868 cooling places and 520 pharmacies; Medicare prescribing by ZIP

Patients (synthetic)

  • 300 patients with 952 medicines, matched to real Bronx health and prescribing rates
  • No real patient data anywhere

Find

From 5.9 million findings to 38 pathways

We asked System for every finding about our medicines, heat, or four body effects: dehydration, low sodium, kidney injury and low blood pressure. We kept the findings that link a medicine or heat to one of those effects, then joined the two sides: medicine → body effect ← heat.

Most of these pathways were never studied as "this drug in the heat". System connects two well-studied halves.

Check

System as a second, independent reader

We read every number we quote from the paper's abstract ourselves. System extracts findings from papers too, so we compared them paper by paper: same number, same meaning, same direction.

Where System read the same paper, it got the same number every time: zero conflicts.

Spot gaps

Where the evidence is thin

Counting System's studies per medicine group shows where the evidence is. Kidney and fluid drugs are well covered. Medicines that stop sweating or raise body heat are almost absent, even though heat guidance worries about them most. Cool-Aid labels those claims "Early signs" instead of overstating them.

Trust

How we keep the evidence honest

  • ✓Every number must appear word for word in its abstract. If it doesn't, the claim is hidden. System can't rescue it.
  • ✓✓Double-verified only when System agrees. 14 numbers. System advises that about 20% of its API extractions are inaccurate; counting only findings also in its curated bulk release, 5 remain. We report both.
  • ⚠A System mistake never downgrades our claim. In 3 papers System had our number but named the wrong drug. Our claim stays ✓, and the error goes to a fix list for System.
  • ↗Support is labeled as support. A System link that agrees with a claim but isn't the same result shows as ↗, never as proof.
  • !No dose advice. No invented data. Every label and number in Cool-Aid comes from a source you can open.
